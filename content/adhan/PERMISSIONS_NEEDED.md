# Adhan recordings that need permission

The openly licensed pool contains **no** recording at the level of the leading apps. Professional muezzin recordings belong to someone, and shipping them needs a written licence. This file lists the exact recordings worth asking for, who owns them, how to reach them (and how sure I am of that), and what the licence must say.

**Honesty notes.** Contact details marked ✅ were read from a public page during this work; ⚠️ means I could not verify an address and you must confirm the right channel. I did not contact anyone. Names of muezzins come from Wikipedia's list and are unverified.

## What every licence must say (use as the checklist for any reply)

1. **Scope:** worldwide, non-exclusive, **commercial use allowed**, for distribution inside the SAKINA app (bundled and downloadable) and offline playback by users.
2. **Rights confirmation:** the grantor owns, or is authorised to license, both the **recording** and the **performance** (the muezzin's rights) — in writing.
3. **Adaptation:** permission to trim, loudness-normalise and re-encode (never to otherwise alter the performance).
4. **Attribution:** the exact credit line to display (muezzin, mosque/institution, recordist).
5. **Duration and revocation terms** (perpetual is best; if time-limited, the end date and what happens to downloaded copies).
6. **The exact files:** title, date, source, duration — so the licence names what it covers.

*Why these are hard:* the muezzins are employees of a state body; recordings of them are typically controlled by it or its broadcaster. Expect this to take weeks and possibly a fee — you did not authorise any payment, so I stopped.

## Checked and **not** usable (so you don't repeat the search)

* Cambridge / World Oral Literature Project Cairo adhan recordings — **CC BY-NC-ND 3.0** (non-commercial, no derivatives) ✅ read on the item page.
* IslamWeb recordings — non-commercial (per another developer's research; not independently verified).
* Collections such as `Kiwifu/adhan-mp3`, assabile.com downloads, Doha/Qatar Internet Archive sets — no licence, or "public domain" marks set by anonymous re-uploaders of living muezzins' recordings.
* "Beautiful adhan" / "Azan" on Commons — the uploader accounts are blocked for abusing multiple accounts ✅ verified in the Commons block log.
* Sabah Fakhri's Commons adhan — invalid public-domain claim (a 1985 recording).
* Muslim Pro and similar apps: their muezzin recordings are their own licensed or commissioned content; I found nothing in public materials that grants reuse.
